What the dates mean — and what we do not promise.

Source as of

Every unit carries as_of: the committer date of the commit we pinned. The column below is the newest one a project has. A 2020 date on an archived repository is not staleness — it is the newest thing upstream published.

Pinned, so it can be re-checked

Every citation resolves to an immutable, re-fetchable reference — a 40-character commit, a published package version, or a dated snapshot of a vendor's own bytes. Nothing points at a moving target, so any claim can be re-fetched and disputed.

🔴 No refresh cadence is claimed

Rebuilds are dated events, recorded in the corpus stamp above — not a schedule. We publish when a project was pinned and when the index was last loaded. We do not promise a next date, because we would not be able to keep it.
