## 2 · The enumeration — 25, not 27, and the two that left

**Vendored wordlist**, committed at `artifacts/wordlist/english-lower.txt` (210,625 lowercase words,
2.2 MB). ⚠ **Its absence throws** rather than producing an empty floor: a missing input that
degrades to permissive is the `3.0.2` placeholder class.

🔵 **The pre-registration said 27; the built floor holds 25.** The two that left are **`morpho`** (a
butterfly genus) and **`sui`** (of *sui generis*) — both appear in the platform dictionary **only as
capitalised entries**, and the vendored list is lowercase-only. That is a definition, not a filter:
the floor is about *ordinary English words*, and an id matching only a proper noun is a name. **A
set that changes size between pre-registration and run must say so.**

**5 of the 25 hold a stamped recipe — `across · dynamic · pimlico · privy · reown` — and those five
are the entire attribution exposure.** The other 20 can only produce off-target units, which is a
ranking cost rather than an attribution one.

## 4 · 🔴 The defect the first build shipped, and the rule it produced

**Deployed `97dc0872`, probed live:** *"send tokens across two chains"* **still earned
`names_project` and still served Across's signed recipe.** The floor was in and it did not fire.

**Cause:** corroboration accepted **any** hit on a different token. The words **`bridge`** and
**`tokens`** are `member` identifiers inside `across`, so the query corroborated the project with
two more ordinary English words.

> ⭐ **A COINCIDENCE CANNOT CORROBORATE A COINCIDENCE.** If the floor exists because an English word
> is not a name, an English word used as a symbol is not evidence either — it is the same
> coincidence one level in. **That is D3 walking back in through the corroboration door.**

**Fixed in two layers, both reusing already-ruled machinery:** a corroborating hit must be a
`declared` kind **or** survive the **shape floor** (F54) and the **share floor** (F56); and it must
not be **word-shaped** — `WORD_SHAPED_IDENTIFIERS`, the 2,735 identifier tokens the corpus holds
that are also dictionary words (~29 KB, generated with the coverage list and re-derived
byte-for-byte by its spec).

⚠ **What still corroborates is `bridgetokens`** — the pair token of a real Across API
(`bridgeTokens`) — which is exactly the *"member identifier from the same project"* the ruling
names. **The floor got sharper; it did not get blunter.**

### 🔵 M1 PUBLISHES AS A PAIR — ruled 2026-08-20, and it changes what this table means

> **`reachable  19 direct + 5 pointed = 24 of 24` · 0 mis-served — ✔ GREEN**

R-G's discipline applied to reachability: **"19/24" is a true number that misleads.** All five
remaining recipes are declined **by ruling** — D1's family for `safe-protocol-kit`, F68's floor for
the other four — and in every case the payload **names the withheld recipe and the argument that
serves it**. A bare fraction reads as five recipes nobody can get to.

`formatReachabilityPair` **throws** rather than rendering a pair that does not reconstruct its
total, and the pointer is read from the **structured** fields (`recipe_candidates`,
`not_indexed_members`), never from prose — a regex over the text half would pass on a sentence that
merely mentions the project.

**M1 before: 23 direct + 1 pointed. After: 19 direct + 5 pointed. F68 moved four from direct to
pointed; none became unreachable.** ⚠ **Every one of the four receives the Q4
pointer** — verified live, one call each — so Q3's condition holds: *the declined case is never a
void.*

⚠ **`across` is the fifth recipe-holding word and it did NOT lose reachability**, because M1's
question for it (*"how do I bridge tokens with across"*) carries `bridgetokens`.

## 8 · Falsifications — five, each shown to land

| break | result |
|---|---|
| remove the floor clause entirely | the bare-`reown` test fails |
| let a dictionary token corroborate itself | the same test fails |
| delete a pair declaration | the alias-set test fails, naming it |
| remove the vendored wordlist | the build **throws**, rather than producing an empty floor |
| ship a pair that does not contain its word | the containment test fails |

🔴 **The third falsification did NOT land at first, and that is recorded.** Deleting the
`reown appkit` declaration left every test green — the serving tests hard-code the identifier row in
their fixture, so **nothing anywhere asserted that the declaration existed**. The set the owner
approved was unprotected. A missing check, not a weak one.

## 9 · One more instrument defect, found by the bar itself

🔴 **M1 was printing a green `11/11` over a corpus of 24 recipes.** Its roster was a hand-written
map, and **this file's own header had warned about exactly that** — *"silently stop covering a
recipe the moment one was added, and '11 of 11' would still print"*. The warning was written and the
mechanism was not built. The roster now **enumerates the recipes table**; curated questions are kept
where they exist and derived ones are labelled as derived.
